# [Example] Sample mean

**In one sentence:** the average of $n$ observations, $\bar x = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$.

## Definition
For i.i.d. samples with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$,
$$
\mathbb{E}[\bar x] = \mu, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(\bar x) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}.
$$
See [[ref:smith2020example]] (example reference).
