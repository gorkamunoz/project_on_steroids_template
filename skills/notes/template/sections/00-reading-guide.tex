\section*{How to read these notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to read these notes}

These notes collect every derivation of the project in one place. They are a working document, not a paper.
Each result carries a status tag in the margin:
\textcolor{ForestGreen}{\textsf{proved}} (the proof is complete and in this document or in a cited reference),
\textcolor{RoyalBlue}{\textsf{numerical}} (supported by experiments, linking to the lab),
\textcolor{BrickRed}{\textsf{conjecture}}, or
\textcolor{Orange}{\textsf{proof needs checking}}.
Line numbers are for review comments (``p.\,3 l.\,25'').
